Before Home Assistant 2026.8, the HTTP integration was configured in __DATA_DIR__/configuration.yaml. The first time Home Assistant starts after you upgrade, your existing http: block is imported into the UI, and a repair issue is raised under Settings > System > Repairs to remind you to manually remove it.

To complete the migration or have more details, see https://www.home-assistant.io/integrations/http/#migrating-from-yaml
